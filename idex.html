Cambodia Independence Day

Cambodia Independence Day is celebrated every year on November 9. It commemorates Cambodia's independence from France on November 9, 1953. King Norodom Sihanouk played an important role in leading Cambodia to independence. On this special day, Cambodian people celebrate by displaying national flags and participating in ceremonies. The King lights the Victory Flame at the Independence Monument in Phnom Penh. People also remember the heroes who contributed to the country's freedom. Independence Day is an important national holiday because it represents freedom, peace, unity and national pride. We should be proud of our country and respect its history.

Happy Independence Day, Cambodia! 🇰🇭

Khmer New Year

Khmer New Year is celebrated in April every year.

People enjoy traditional games, dancing, and visiting pagodas.

Chum Ben

Pchum Ben is a traditional festival to remember deceased ancestors.

People bring food to pagodas to offer to monks and their ancestors.

Water Festival (Bon Om Touk)

The Water Festival celebrates the changing flow of the Tonle Sap River.

People enjoy exciting boat races, fireworks, and celebrations.

Visak Bochea

Visak Bochea celebrates the birth, enlightenment, and passing away of Buddha.

People visit temples and participate in religious ceremonies.

Meak Bochea

Meak Bochea is an important Buddhist festival in Cambodia.

Buddhists visit pagodas, pray, and listen to Buddha's teachings.

Angkor Sankranta

Angkor Sankranta is a special celebration during Khmer New Year in Siem Reap.

People enjoy traditional games, cultural performances, and Khmer food.
